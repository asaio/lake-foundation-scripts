My Journey

From Individual Contributor to Junior Engineering Manager

Exploring growth, feedback, and leadership evolution.

Individual Contributor

- Focused on technical excellence and delivery.
- Held myself to high standards.
- Received feedback: sometimes applied those standards too rigidly to others.

Tech Lead of a Squad

- Applied situational leadership.
- Mentored two engineers from junior to senior.
- Supported diverse contributions.
- Feedback: supportive, approachable.

Transition to Junior Engineering Manager

- Guided by Start With Why.
- Using First 90 Days STARS framework.
- Sustaining Success & Realignment.
- Develop people actively, provide actionable feedback.

Why Management Made Sense

- Desired to define processes and strategies.
- Mentored engineers to confidence and performance.
- Helped diverse contributors thrive.
- Realignment needed for low NPS.
- Management multiplies impact through people.

Conflict Management Skills

Internal: Investigated sexism concerns, coached active listening, improved dynamics.
External: Realigned supplier expectations, clarified SLOs.
Cross‑Functional: Resolved misunderstanding with cloud team, restored capability.
Outcome: Clear communication and resolution across contexts.

First 90 Days Virtuous Cycles

- Diagnose: Don’t rush to act.
- Establish Relationships: Build trust with stakeholders, clients, peers.
- Seek Quick Wins: Achieve early credibility.
- Gain Credibility: Deliver consistently.
- Virtuous Cycle: Each success builds momentum.
- Outcome: Smooth transition balancing sustaining success and realignment.

Vision Forward

- Multiply impact through people.
- Balance standards with empathy.
- Foster growth, accountability, innovation.
- Deliver with excellence and satisfaction.
